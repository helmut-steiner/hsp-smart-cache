# Security Policy

Use a private reporting channel expressly designated by Helmut Steiner in the [Original Project](https://github.com/helmut-steiner/hsp-smart-cache).

If no private channel is available, open a [non-sensitive contact request](https://github.com/helmut-steiner/hsp-smart-cache/issues) asking Helmut Steiner for a private delivery method. Do not put vulnerability details, exploit code, credentials, signing keys, or private site data in the public request. Wait for private delivery instructions before sending sensitive material.

For modifications covered by the [HSP Personal Noncommercial Contribution License 1.0](LICENSE), section 4 provides the safe-delivery procedure. Keep a dated record of your request or attempted delivery and a submission-ready copy while a channel is unavailable. Check the documented contact locations at least every 30 days and deliver within 14 days after you learn, or would learn through those checks, that safe delivery is available. Do not disclose sensitive details publicly merely to meet a contribution deadline.
