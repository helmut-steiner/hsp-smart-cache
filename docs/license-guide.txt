Version 1.0 was adopted for HSP Smart Cache on 5 November 2026.
The authoritative project terms are in LICENSE. The complete reusable text in
docs/license-template.txt is an identical copy. This guide explains those terms
and their reuse; it is not part of the operative license.

The license uses "this software" rather than a product name. It names Helmut
Steiner as copyright owner and Licensor and requires no software-name, URL,
or contact placeholders. Its HSP name identifies the license, not the software
covered by it.

REUSING THE LICENSE FOR YOUR OTHER SOFTWARE AND PLUGINS

  1. Copy the complete docs/license-template.txt text into the other project's
     LICENSE file. Check that you have the rights necessary to license the
     software under these terms and retain all third-party licensing notices.
  2. Check the copyright years against that project's actual history and update
     the notice where appropriate. A different owner's project also requires
     changing the copyright-owner and Licensor notices.
  3. Identify that software's Original Project in its README, official
     documentation, website, or package metadata. Supply a working contribution
     channel and a private security-reporting contact or a documented way to
     request private delivery. These project details belong in the software's
     documentation rather than in license placeholders.
  4. Retain those upstream details with redistributed copies. A fork cannot
     redirect the contribution obligation to itself, and reusing this license
     does not route unrelated software contributions to HSP Smart Cache.
  5. Use "HSP Personal Noncommercial Contribution License 1.0" consistently in
     project notices. Where supported, add a License URI pointing to that
     project's adopted LICENSE file. Explain the contribution requirements and
     the rights acknowledgment in its contribution instructions.

The wording covers applications, command-line tools, libraries, plugins, and
other software, as well as websites. Ordinary settings, user data, and output
are excluded from the contribution requirement. Transformed or generated
copies of the Software itself are not exempt merely because they are output.

POLICY SUMMARY

  - Personal noncommercial use by individuals is permitted. Business, client,
    organizational, advertising, affiliate, and fundraising uses require
    separate written permission. Ordinary hosting costs and donations to the
    Licensor are allowed under the stated conditions.
  - Substantive modifications, including private, experimental, unfinished,
    and later abandoned changes, must be submitted within 30 calendar days
    of first saving each substantive change, or before redistribution,
    whichever is earlier. Transient editor states are not required.
  - Eligible noncommercial forks may be shared for free with source and notices.
  - Contributors retain their copyright and grant the Licensor the section 5
    rights, including commercial and proprietary relicensing rights. This is
    a license of their rights, not a transfer of ownership or a promise to
    merge a patch.
  - Section 4 provides safe-delivery suspensions when a channel is unavailable
    or private delivery cannot yet be arranged. Contributors must keep dated
    records and the patch, check contact locations at least every 30 days,
    and deliver within 14 days after learning, or when required checks would
    reveal, that safe delivery is available.

    license. Third-party terms and mandatory statutory rights remain intact.
